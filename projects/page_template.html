Industry Projects

Ten end-to-end projects modelled on real data jobs. Each has a business brief, realistic data, 6–8 milestones whose answers are checked automatically, hints, a hidden reference solution, a report template, resume bullets and the interview questions you'll be asked about it. Open any of them in Google Colab — nothing to install.

  1. Run the first cell, read the brief, and work milestone by milestone until every check shows ✅.
  2. Write the short report at the end in your own words.
  3. Publish the notebook on your GitHub with a README: problem, data, approach, results, next steps.
